Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about eligibility, the degree, and life at Tensor.

Is the degree valid for Govt Jobs or MS Abroad?+−

Yes. You receive a UG degree in Computer Science & AI from S-VYASA deemed to be University, which is UGC-Recognized and AICTE-Approved. It is fully valid for UPSC, GATE, and Masters degrees in the US/UK.

Do I need prior coding experience?+−

No. Our curriculum starts from the absolute basics (Logic & Syntax) in Year 1. We select based on mathematical aptitude and problem-solving potential, not prior coding knowledge.

Is the hostel mandatory?+−

Yes. Tensor is a fully residential program. We believe the most valuable learning happens during late-night hackathons and peer collaboration in the Hacker House.

Are education loans available?+−

Yes. We have partnerships with leading financial institutions to facilitate education loans for eligible students. Our admissions team guides you through the documentation.

What if I don't clear the T-SAT?+−

Admission is strictly merit-based. If you don't clear the first round, you may apply for the next cycle, but standards remain the same.
