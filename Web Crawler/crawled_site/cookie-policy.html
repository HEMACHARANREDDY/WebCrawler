Company: Upskill Leverage Private Limited (Operating as "Tensor School of CS & AI")

1. What the Hell is a Cookie?

Let's keep this simple. A cookie is a small text file that our website places on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit tensorschool.com. These files allow us to recognize your browser, remember your preferences, keep your session secure during checkout, and track how you interact with our site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We don't use cookies to be malicious; we use them to make sure the website actually works and to ensure our marketing budget isn't being set on fire. We use the following types of cookies:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are non-negotiable. Without these, you cannot log in, apply to the 4-year UG program, or securely process payments via Razorpay. You cannot opt out of these if you want the site to function.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This tells us which pages are popular, how long people stay, and where they drop off. This data is aggregated and anonymous.

Targeting and Advertising Cookies

We use the Meta Pixel. If you look at our course curriculum and leave without applying, this pixel allows us to show you targeted advertisements for Tensor School on Facebook and Instagram.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you use our site, you may also receive cookies from our third-party service providers. We do not control these cookies.

  • Razorpay: Places essential security and session cookies when you are paying your application or tuition fees.
  • Partner Universities: If our site embeds portals or links to our university partners, they may place their own cookies.

4. How to Control or Delete Cookies

You are in control of your browser. You have the right to accept or reject cookies.

  • Browser Settings: You can modify your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block or delete cookies.
  • The Catch: If you choose to disable all cookies (including Strictly Necessary ones), parts of tensorschool.com will break. You likely won't be able to submit an application or complete a payment.
  • Opting out of targeted ads: You can opt out of Meta's targeted advertising by adjusting your ad preferences directly within your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

5. Updates to this Policy

Technology changes, and so do the trackers we use. We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our tracking practices or legal obligations. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
